How a GPU turns triangles into pixels

Follow one frame through the real-time render pipeline. The course starts with the classical foundations: transforms, rasterization, shading and texturing. It then moves through the programmable GPU and modern techniques, and ends with the explicit APIs and game engines that ship games today. Every step has a visualization you can drag, slide and break.

How to use this course

  1. Read the chapters in order. Each one assumes the last, and the overview diagram above shows where each chapter sits in the pipeline.
  2. Touch every visualization. Drag the vertices and lights, push the sliders to their extremes, and watch what breaks.
  3. Keep the conventions in mind. Matrices are OpenGL-style column-major (P · V · M), the world is right-handed and y-up, and the camera looks down −Z.
  4. After Chapter 09, try building a tiny software rasterizer or a WebGPU triangle yourself. The chapters give you every piece.
